# Home surface

Mode: Operate. Users repeatedly open an app, read a notification, or find an app with one hand.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Niagara's vertical favorites and alphabetical navigation, explicitly chosen by the user.
OWN-WORLD: Transparent launcher over the system wallpaper with user-controlled dimming from 0% to 100%, warm white text, native app icons, quiet native sheets. Long-press management uses a deep navy surface and pale blue contour icons matching the supplied reference, an app icon/title header whose title opens rename, inline additional actions, and a separated settings entry. Its window enters from below in 300ms and exits in 180ms, honoring disabled system animations.
STORY: Open an app directly, drag the alphabet to find another, swipe an app for its pop-up.
